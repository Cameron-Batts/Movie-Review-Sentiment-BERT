Movie Review Sentiment Classification with BERT

Problem
Classifying short movie reviews requires models that recognize sentiment beyond isolated word counts.
Approach
In a collaborative project, compared unigram/bigram Naive Bayes and logistic regression with fully fine-tuned BERT using predefined Rotten Tomatoes training, validation and test splits.
Impact
Traditional baselines reproduced 77.77% and 76.36% accuracy. The 84.80% BERT result remains historical. A seeded MPS/CUDA workflow is prepared; its new training run is pending.
